# SAPTARISHI Photography Prompts & Art Direction

All imagery follows the strict Saptarishi art direction:

Prefix for every prompt:
`Ultra-luxury hotel photography, warm amber architectural lighting at dusk, deep obsidian shadows, bronze and dark marble, linen tones, cinematic, photorealistic, calm and quiet, no people, no text, no logos, no watermarks.`

### Spaces (Spaces to linger)
- **lobby.webp**: Grand double-height lobby, bronze-framed glass, long dark marble reception desk, amber ceiling cove lighting, calm and expansive.
- **dining.webp**: Intimate fine dining room with seven candlelit tables, dark linen and crystal, floor-to-ceiling silk curtains, glowing amber lighting.
- **spa.webp**: Serene thermal spa sanctuary, dark warm stone, still reflecting water pool, gentle ambient steam, quiet candles.
- **studio.webp**: Private minimalist training studio with dark wood floor, bronze details, large floor-to-ceiling glass window with soft blue dusk light.
- **pool.webp**: Heated infinity pool at dusk reflecting deep twilight sky, minimal dark stone deck, private cabanas with soft amber glow.

### Signature Suites (Seven suites. Seven skies.)
- **suite-1.webp** (Surya Suite): Low bed with linen and umber textiles, dark bronze wall panelling, marble accents, private sunrise terrace view.
- **suite-2.webp** (Chandra Suite / Tara Suite): Penthouse suite with private plunge pool on a dark marble terrace overlooking the twilight skyline.
- **suite-3.webp** (Akasha Suite / Aruna Suite): Panoramic suite with floor-to-ceiling glass wall sky view of twilight clouds and distant horizon, low luxury sofa and bronze details.
- **suite-4.webp** (Nakshatra Suite / Sapta Suite): Minimalist rooftop terrace residence suite at twilight, daybed, bronze fire pit, city lights in the distance.

### Moments (Moments you remember)
- **moment-arrival.webp**: A dark luxury limousine at a glowing bronze-framed architectural entrance, wet marble reflections.
- **moment-chef.webp**: An intimate chef's table prepared for private dining with candlelight, bronze utensils, a few steps from open kitchen.
- **moment-sunset.webp**: Rooftop sky lounge at sunset, warm amber twilight glow, low lounge seating overlooking the horizon.
- **moment-spa.webp**: Thermal stone spa bath with candles, still dark water, quiet steam, warm architectural shadows.
